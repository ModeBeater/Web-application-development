How tagging works

Each topic (title + keywords + section text) is a document. A new question is compared with every topic by TF-IDF over character n-grams (cosine similarity) plus a bonus for matching keywords; the best topic is used when the score reaches the threshold, otherwise the question stays untagged. The same similarity shows “already asked” hints while a student types.
